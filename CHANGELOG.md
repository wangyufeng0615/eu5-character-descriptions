# Changelog

## 1.0.9 — 2026-11-14

- Other game languages (French, German, Spanish and more) now show the English biographies. Based on player feedback.

## 1.0.8 — 2026-11-13

- Revised from feedback by Tricolore Royal. Thank you! Guillaume III Roger (born 1332) is now correctly described as the younger brother of Pope Gregory XI in Chinese, and the Roger family entries use the more common Chinese form of Gregory. John V Palaiologos: the 1347 co-emperorship is explained more clearly in Chinese. Maria-Irene, John V's elder sister, was wrongly called his younger sister and mixed up with another Maria who married Francesco Gattilusio; her entry now says she became a nun at Mesembria after being widowed in 1355 and was buried there around 1399 (both languages).

## 1.0.7 — 2026-11-12

- Biographies now contain links: hover a person, place, country, dynasty, culture or religion mentioned in a biography to open its own tooltip, including ancestors who died before 1337. The biography now appears in the tooltip right away instead of after a short delay.

## 1.0.6 — 2026-11-12

- Mostly Chinese text: a second pass made names consistent, covering short forms and family members missed in 1.0.5, with one spelling per family and place names inside names matching the game's own; also corrected Margaret of Lusignan's marriage year (both languages).

## 1.0.5 — 2026-11-11

- Chinese text only: each person is now named consistently across all Chinese biographies (over a thousand entries standardized), and stray traditional characters were converted to simplified Chinese.

## 1.0.4 — 2026-11-10

- Biographies are now split into two or three paragraphs and tightened by about 15 percent; every rewritten entry was checked against the previous text and a number of small errors were corrected.

## 1.0.3 — 2026-11-05

- Long biographies now scroll inside the character tooltip, so the tooltip no longer runs off the bottom of the screen.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-11-05

- Researched 208 rulers again, turning 105 short or missing entries into full biographies; event characters such as Wang Yangming now show a biography; fixed errors logged when hovering characters.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-04

- Rewrote the biographies of 663 key rulers, heirs and regents of the 1337 start.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04

- First public release.
