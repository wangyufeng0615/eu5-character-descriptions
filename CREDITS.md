# Sources and credits

`sources/sources.jsonl` (in the mod) and the `sources` field of `data/bios.jsonl` (in this repository) list, for each character, the pages consulted when writing the biography. Most are Wikipedia articles in English or the local language; Wikipedia contributors are credited through those pages and their history. The list records research provenance; it does not mean Wikipedia or any other source endorses this mod or that every statement was independently verified.

The biographies condense, reorganise and translate consulted material with AI assistance, and the source pages are not reproduced.
