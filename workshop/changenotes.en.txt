Guillaume III Roger (born 1332) is now correctly described as the younger brother of Pope Gregory XI in Chinese, and the Roger family entries use the more common Chinese form of Gregory. John V Palaiologos: the 1347 co-emperorship is explained more clearly in Chinese. Maria-Irene, John V's elder sister, was wrongly called his younger sister and mixed up with another Maria who married Francesco Gattilusio; her entry now says she became a nun at Mesembria after being widowed in 1355 and was buried there around 1399 (both languages).
