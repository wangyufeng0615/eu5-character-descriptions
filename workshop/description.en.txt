[h1]A biography for every historical character[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10636967561533810313/8AF99E1168E750BB2B2D761375ECE46DF5E5F35E/[/img]

Vanilla EU5 tells you a character's traits and stats, but not who they were.

This mod adds English and Chinese biographies to [b]7,789[/b] historical characters, [b]90%[/b] of all those in the game: the rulers, consorts and heirs of the 1337 start, their ancestors in the family tree, and famous people who arrive through events. Hover over a character and the biography appears at the bottom of the tooltip.

[h2]Who you'll meet[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Trasak Paem[/b], a royal gardener in Cambodia who guarded the king's cucumbers so fiercely that he speared the king himself, and was later chosen as king by a white elephant
[*][b]Olga of Kiev[/b], who avenged her husband by burning the Drevlian capital with birds carrying burning sulphur
[*][b]Qutb ud-Din Aibak[/b], a slave sold in the market of Nishapur who founded the Delhi Sultanate, then died when his saddle pierced his ribs in a polo match
[*][b]Charlemagne[/b], who received an elephant named Abul-Abbas from the caliph Harun al-Rashid
[*][b]Valdemar II[/b] of Denmark, at whose battle of Lindanise, legend says, the Danish flag fell from the sky
[*]And Constantine the Great, Zheng He, Wang Yangming, Napoleon…
[/list]

[list]
[*]English and Chinese in one mod
[*]Real lives: origins, key years, what they did and how they died
[*]Covers the dead too: open a family tree and the ancestors have biographies as well
[*]Only adds text; no gameplay changes
[*]Can be added to an existing save
[/list]

Every biography is written from published sources, mainly Wikipedia in English and the local language, and matched to the game's character by family, dates and titles. The 663 rulers, heirs and regents alive in 1337 were rewritten a second time to show the world they lived in and the deeds they are remembered for. Where little is known about a person, the biography stays short rather than invented. I used the best models available and spent a lot of tokens on this, and I think it was worth it :)

If you find a mistake, please leave a comment with the character's name. I'll keep maintaining and updating this mod!

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Most characters created by events have no fixed ID in the game. A few famous ones, such as Wang Yangming, are recognised by name and birthplace; about 420 others don't show a biography yet.
[*]Randomly generated characters have no biography.
[*]Mods that replace the character tooltip may hide the biography.
[*]Biographies tell the person's real life and do not change with what happens in your game.
[/list]
