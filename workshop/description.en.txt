[h1]A biography for every historical character[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10300716693799460515/2B2DD8B3A388ED947512F400327F3F5E7646FBCB/[/img]

Vanilla EU5 has a great many real historical characters and rich family trees, but very few character descriptions.

This mod adds English and Chinese biographies to [b]7,789[/b] real historical characters, nearly all of those in the game. Hover over a character to read it.

For every character, famous or obscure, I gathered sources, mainly Wikipedia in English and the local language, and matched them to the game's character by family, dates and titles. Each biography tells the world the person lived in and what they are remembered for. When little is known about someone, the biography stays short; nothing is made up.

About the use of AI: models such as GPT 6.1 Terra and DeepSeek Flash gathered the sources, then Claude Opus 5.5, one of the best models for writing, combined and polished them in the spirit of EU5. These are the best models available and it took a huge number of tokens, but I think it was worth it :)

[*][b]Trasak Paem[/b]: a royal gardener in Cambodia who guarded the king's cucumbers so well that he fatally speared the king himself when he came to test him at night, and was later chosen as king by a white elephant
[*][b]Olga of Kiev[/b]: avenged her husband by burning the Drevlian capital with birds carrying burning sulphur
[*][b]Qutb ud-Din Aibak[/b]: a boy sold in the slave market of Nishapur who went on to found the Delhi Sultanate, and died when his saddle pierced his ribs in a polo match
[*][b]Charlemagne[/b]: the caliph Harun al-Rashid sent him an elephant named Abul-Abbas
[*][b]Valdemar II of Denmark[/b]: legend says the Danish flag fell from the sky at his victory at Lindanise
[*]And Constantine the Great, Zheng He, Wang Yangming, Napoleon…
[/list]
