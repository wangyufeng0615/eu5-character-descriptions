[h1]A biography for every historical character[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10300716693799460515/2B2DD8B3A388ED947512F400327F3F5E7646FBCB/[/img]

Vanilla EU5 has a great many real historical characters and full family trees, but very few character descriptions. This mod adds English and Chinese biographies to [b]7,789[/b] real historical characters, nearly all of those in the game. Hover over a character to read it. Biographies draw mainly on Wikipedia in English and the local language; when little is known, they stay short rather than make things up.

⭐ [b]Strongly recommended: subscribe to the other two EU5 mods as well.[/b] Together the three cover the map, its people and its countries.
