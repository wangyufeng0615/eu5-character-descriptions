# Descriptions for Every Historical Character

English and Simplified Chinese biographies for 7,747 historical characters in Europa Universalis V. Hover over a character's portrait; the biography is at the bottom of the tooltip. The game language picks English or Chinese.

Manual install: extract the release ZIP into `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Europa Universalis V\mod\` so that you have `mod\character_descriptions\` (keep the hidden `.metadata` folder), then enable the mod in Mods and DLC. Do not enable a manual copy and the Workshop copy together.

The biographies were written with AI assistance from published sources, mostly Wikipedia, matched to the game's characters and reviewed entry by entry. Corrections are welcome in the Workshop comments. Biography text is CC BY-SA 4.0, tools are MIT; see LICENSE and CREDITS.md. Per-character sources are in `sources/sources.jsonl`.
